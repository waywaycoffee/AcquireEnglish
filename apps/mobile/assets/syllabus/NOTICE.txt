Syllabus word books (CET4/CET6 core)
====================================

These bundled lists are frequency-band subsets derived from the project's
local ECDICT core asset for study UX. They are NOT official Ministry of
Education syllabus transcripts and must not be marketed as such.

Chinese glosses come from the same offline dictionary pipeline already
shipped with the app. Rebuild with:

  python scripts/build_syllabus_books.py

Optional online catalog (user-initiated download only)
------------------------------------------------------
The in-app “在线词库” browser fetches lists from the community repo
https://github.com/lilinji/English on demand. Files are stored locally as
user word books. Content copyright remains with original authors /
publishers; personal study use only — do not redistribute commercially.
